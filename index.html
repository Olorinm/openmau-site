Mau

AI employees for your own work.

What Mau is

Mau lets you create AI employees and talk with them in the Mau web console, Feishu or Matrix. Each employee works in its own isolated environment and carries out the tasks you give it.

You can connect apps an employee may use for you, such as Gmail or a browser. You choose which employee can use which connection. Credentials stay in Mau's connector service; the employee never receives them. Before an employee changes anything outside Mau — sending, drafting, editing, uploading — you approve that exact action on a card.

Gmail access is read-only: an employee can search and read your mail when you ask it to, for example to summarize what arrived today.

Read how Mau handles your data in the privacy policy.

Mau 是什么

Mau 让你创建 AI 员工，并在 Mau 网页控制台、飞书或 Matrix 里和它们对话。每个员工在自己独立的环境里工作，完成你交代的任务。

你可以给员工连接它能替你使用的应用，比如 Gmail 或浏览器，并决定哪个员工能用哪个连接。授权凭证保存在 Mau 的连接器服务里，员工拿不到。员工在 Mau 之外做任何改动（发送、起草、编辑、上传）之前，都要你在卡片上批准这一具体操作。

Gmail 只读：只有在你要求时，员工才会搜索和阅读你的邮件，比如总结今天收到的邮件。

数据如何处理，请看隐私政策。
